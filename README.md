## About the Movie

**Ready Player One** is a science fiction and adventure movie released in *2018* and directed by **Steven Spielberg**. The story takes place in the future, where people escape their everyday lives by entering a virtual reality world called the `OASIS`.

The main character, Wade Watts, joins a competition to find a hidden Easter egg. Whoever finds it will gain control of the entire OASIS.

## Main Characters

| Character | Description |
|:---|:---|
| **Wade Watts (Parzival)** | The main character who searches for the hidden Easter egg. |
| **Samantha Cook (Art3mis)** | A skilled player who becomes Wade's ally. |
| **James Halliday** | The creator of the OASIS. |
| **Nolan Sorrento** | A businessman who wants to take control of the OASIS. |

## Why I Like This Movie

I like this movie because it combines technology, adventure, and imagination. It also shows how teamwork, friendship, and determination can help people overcome difficult challenges.

Some things I enjoy about the movie:

- The storyline is exciting and unpredictable.
- The virtual world is creative and interesting.
- The characters have different skills and personalities.
- It includes references to classic games and movies.

## My Favorite Part

My favorite part is when Wade and his friends work together to complete the challenges inside the OASIS. I enjoy seeing how they use their knowledge and skills to solve problems. It reminds me that teamwork and believing in yourself are important.
